Before you begin

The details that matter when you are building a daily study routine.

Is this an official JLPT vocabulary test?

No. Language Finds is an independent practice tool. N5–N1 labels help organize study but do not represent official JLPT vocabulary lists.

How do the 20-word units work?

Each level is divided into fixed groups of up to 20 words. Language Finds continues from the first word in your current unit that has not yet been studied.

When will a word return for review?

Again starts with 10 minutes, Hard starts with tomorrow, and Know starts with three days. Later intervals grow with your review history.

What happens to words I miss in a quiz?

They are added to Smart Review automatically. When space remains, a missed word may also return later in the same 10-question round.

Where is my progress saved?

On this device only. No account is required, and no study history is sent to a server. Clearing browser storage will also clear your progress.

What does the AI story feature do?

It creates a short story containing all ten words from your completed round. It is optional and limited separately from the browser-local study tools.
